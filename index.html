How to play
  • Place your 5 ships on a 10×10 grid (lengths 5, 4, 3, 3, 2).
  • Take turns firing one shot at your opponent's grid.
  • When every cell of a ship is hit, it sinks.
  • The first to sink the whole enemy fleet wins.
